## Longer version (for forum/long-form channel)

**Introducing ESO Toolkit — a community combat log analyzer for ESO**

Hey all,

I've been working on a tool called **ESO Toolkit** that I'd love to share with the guild.
It's a free, web-based combat log analyzer aimed at making ESO logs easier to dig through —
whether you're progging a hard mode trial, debugging a tricky mechanic, or just curious
why your parse dipped on that last pull.

**Highlights:**

- 🎬 **Fight Replay** — a 3D playback of the encounter. Scrub the timeline, rotate the
  camera, and watch positioning, mechanics, and cooldowns play out at 60fps.
- 📊 **Parse Analysis** — DPS/HPS numbers, ability breakdowns, uptime tracking, and
  player-vs-player comparisons.
- 🛡️ **Loadout Manager** — gear sets, skill bars, and champion points per player.
- ✍️ **Scribing Support** — detects signature scripts across every event type
  (including resource events), so Scribing skills show up correctly in the breakdown.
- 🧭 **Role Detection** — tanks, healers, and damage dealers are classified automatically
  from their actual combat behavior, not just their class.
- 👥 **Roster Hub** — manage your trial/dungeon rosters in one place.

**Live site:** https://esotk.com/
**Source / issues:** https://github.com/ESO-Toolkit/eso-toolkit

It's still actively being developed, so if you run into something odd or have ideas for
features you'd like to see, let me know — guild feedback would be hugely appreciated.

Thanks, and happy logging!
