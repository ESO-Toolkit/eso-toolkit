Why clip-path, not width: width is a layout property, so the spring animation forced a reflow every frame; clip-path composites. Negative insets on the three static sides (-12px) keep the fill's outer glow un-clipped — clip-path clips the element's own box-shadow — and round 4px preserves the rounded cap at the moving edge (AttributeBar.tsx:149-154).
Overflow negotiation rule: a surface keeps overflow: hidden when it paints decorations that must stay inside its rounded corners (GlassPanel / glassCardSurface top strips, the expanded replay deck's corner glow), but must switch to overflow: visible when children render beyond its edge — the compact replay overlay's scrub-preview bubble (replayDesign.ts:135-139) and the top-DPS card's negative-inset electric ring + halo (PlayerCard.tsx:601-605).
