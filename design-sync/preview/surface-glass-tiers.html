Ladder vs. source: the catalog's "hero/tooltip 22–24" tier refers to hero surfaces like the HeaderBar dropdown (blur(24px)); the tooltips themselves measure blur(16px) saturate(1.4) in source. The dialog tier spans 10 (PickerDialog, perf-reduced) to 20 (global). The low-perf axis (html[data-perf='low']) strips every backdrop-filter app-wide — src/index.css:136-140. Light-mode alerts use different rgb bases than dark (e.g. warning 249,115,22 vs 255,152,0).
