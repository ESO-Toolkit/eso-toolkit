Notes: G9 is the canonical copy; near-duplicates live in the roster-hub / build-hub / pack-hub FilterBars. The recessed-well rule (B11 inversion): inputs shade inward (inset 0 2px 4px or a dark fill like rgba(0,0,0,0.22)) while panels highlight their top edge. Saturate appears in two dialects — unitless saturate(1.4) (tooltips, scribing) vs percentage saturate(140%) (TextEditor) — document, don't unify. The panel's borderRadius: 3 resolves to 30px under the theme's shape base 10; the in-source comment calling it a "24px glass panel" (latestReportsStyles.ts:59) is stale. The prompt-cited path for the editor is actually src/components/TextEditor.tsx, not a build-editor primitive.
